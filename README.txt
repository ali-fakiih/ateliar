ATELIER FRAME — PREMIUM PHOTOGRAPHY PORTFOLIO

HOW TO USE
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in a browser, or upload the entire folder to your hosting provider.
3. Keep the assets folder beside index.html, style.css and script.js.

IMAGE RELIABILITY & PERFORMANCE
- Portfolio and journal photos load lazily and decode asynchronously.
- Remote photo requests use more efficient image widths and compression.
- Every photo slot has a bundled local SVG fallback, so broken/blocked photo URLs do not leave blank spaces.
- Hero and decorative background images are checked and replaced by bundled artwork if the remote host fails.
- The bundled fallback artwork works offline. The photographic images still require an internet connection.

CUSTOMIZE
- Replace the sample photographer name, email, prices, testimonials and social links in index.html.
- Replace the Unsplash image URLs with your own optimized photography when ready.
- Contact form opens the visitor's default email app; connect a form backend for direct submissions.
